<objective>
Prove, on real CI runs, the three things that cannot be proven locally: that the matrix actually runs and
passes on all four Node majors, that a lint failure actually fails the job, and — the single most important
proof in this phase — that the Node 26 leg would have caught the original bug.

Purpose: a workflow only runs from the branch it exists on, so every SIG-03 assertion up to now has been
static YAML parsing. Static parsing cannot tell you that `setup-node` resolves Node 26, that ESLint 10's
engine range is satisfied on the Node 20 leg, that the artifact upload does not 409, or that the matrix
discriminates by version at all. Without SIG-03f in particular, "the blind spot is closed" is an unverified
claim — and it is exactly the kind of awkward manual proof that gets quietly skipped.

Output: three recorded CI runs with URLs and per-leg outcomes, two scratch branches created and destroyed,
and the branch-protection required-check names brought in line with the renamed job (or the gap explicitly
recorded as a blocker).

branches that must be deleted, locally and remotely, before the plan is complete.
